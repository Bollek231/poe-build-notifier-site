Bot features

How does PoE Build Notifier work?

The bot uses public data from YouTube Data API v3 to find new Path of Exile 1 build guides and share links to original videos on Discord. It does not download or re-upload videos.

2 h

Scanning

Automatically searches every two hours. Administrators can also trigger searches manually.

24 h

Fresh results

Filters videos by age, league, Path of Exile version, and similarity.

20:00

Daily digest

Summarizes builds already posted on Discord at 8:00 PM Warsaw time, without extra YouTube search calls.

Build notifications

Messages include the video title, creator, publication date, and link to the original YouTube video. When matching links are found in the video description, clickable Path of Building, Maxroll, and Mobalytics buttons appear below the message.

Quota protection

When the daily YouTube search quota runs out, the bot shows a concise message with the expected reset time based on Pacific Time and pauses additional searches. Scheduled scans resume after the quota reset.

Privacy and retention

Locally cached YouTube metadata is cleared from SQLite after 7 days. Discord notifications remain in the channel and are not automatically deleted. Accepting the terms applies to administrative commands only; it does not modify channel permissions.

Build classification and duplicate detection use heuristics, so the bot may sometimes exclude a valid guide or consider two different variants similar. Keeping YouTube metadata indefinitely in Discord messages requires a separate compliance assessment under YouTube API policies.
